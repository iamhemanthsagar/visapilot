## 1. Statutory Provisions

### INA §203(b)(1)(B), 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(B)
- **Authority:** Primary Statute
- **Title:** Outstanding Professors and Researchers
- **Citation:** 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(B)
- **Key Terms:**
  - Internationally recognized as outstanding in a specific academic area;
  - At least 3 years of experience in teaching or research in the academic area;
  - Seeks to enter the U.S. for a tenured/tenure-track teaching position, comparable research position at a university or institute of higher education, or comparable research position with a qualifying private employer with at least 3 full-time research employees and documented accomplishments.

---

## 2. Federal Regulations

### 8 CFR §204.5(i) — Outstanding Professors and Researchers
- **Authority:** Binding Federal Regulation
- **Citation:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)
- **Key Subsections:**
  - `8 CFR §204.5(i)(1)`: Eligibility and petition requirements.
  - `8 CFR §204.5(i)(2)`: Definitions (Academic field, Permanent research position).
  - `8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)`: Initial evidentiary criteria (A through F — at least two required).
  - `8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(ii)`: 3-year experience requirement and rules regarding degree-related experience.
  - `8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(iii)`: Offer of employment / employer requirements.

---

## 3. USCIS Policy Manual

### USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 6, Part F, Chapter 3
- **Authority:** USCIS Administrative Policy
- **Title:** Outstanding Professors and Researchers (EB-1B)
- **Key Principles:**
  - Two-step adjudication framework (*Kazarian* analysis applied to EB-1B):
    - Step 1: Objective evaluation of whether evidence satisfies at least two of the six regulatory evidentiary criteria, plus experience and job offer requirements.
    - Step 2: Final merits determination of whether the beneficiary is internationally recognized as outstanding in the academic field.
