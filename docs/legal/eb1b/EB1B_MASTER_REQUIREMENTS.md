## 1. Regulatory Foundation

VisaPilot's EB-1B model is derived strictly from:
- **INA §203(b)(1)(B)**, 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(B)
- **8 CFR §204.5(i)**
- **USCIS Policy Manual**, Volume 6, Part F, Chapter 3 (*Outstanding Professors and Researchers*)
- Relevant binding precedent (*Kazarian v. USCIS*, 596 F.3d 1115 (9th Cir. 2010))

## 3. Threshold / Mandatory Requirements

Unlike EB-1A (which allows self-petitioning and has no rigid 3-year experience rule), EB-1B imposes strict statutory threshold prerequisites:

### B7: Qualifying Experience Requirement (3 Years)
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(ii)
- **Rule:** The beneficiary must possess at least **3 years** of experience in teaching and/or research in the academic field.
- **Propositions:**
  - `B7-P1`: Candidate has accumulated at least 3 years (36 months) of teaching or research experience in the academic field.
  - `B7-P2`: Pre-degree teaching experience counts *only if* the degree was acquired and the candidate had full responsibility for the class taught (instructor of record).
  - `B7-P3`: Pre-degree research experience counts *only if* the degree was acquired and the research has been recognized as outstanding in the academic field.
  - `B7-P4`: Experience is substantiated by letters from current/former employers detailing dates, duties, and terms.

### B8: Qualifying Permanent U.S. Employment Offer
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(iii), 8 CFR §204.5(i)(2)
- **Rule:** The petition must be filed by a qualifying U.S. employer offering a permanent research or tenured/tenure-track teaching position.
- **Propositions:**
  - `B8-P1`: Formal job offer exists from a prospective U.S. employer.
  - `B8-P2`: The offered position is either:
    1. Tenured or tenure-track teaching position at a university/institution of higher education; or
    2. Permanent research position at a university/institution of higher education; or
    3. Permanent research position with a qualifying private employer.
  - `B8-P3`: If with a private employer, the employer employs at least 3 full-time researchers and demonstrates documented accomplishments in an academic field.
  - `B8-P4`: The position is permanent (tenured, tenure-track, or of indefinite duration with reasonable expectation of continued funding).

### B9: Academic Field Context & Scope
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(2)
- **Rule:** The proposed work and documented accomplishments must align with a specific academic field (body of knowledge, discipline, or area of study).
- **Propositions:**
  - `B9-P1`: A specific academic field/discipline is defined and established.
  - `B9-P2`: The candidate's past experience, evidence, and proposed U.S. work fall within this academic field.

## 4. Initial Evidentiary Criteria (B1 through B6)

Under 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i), the petitioner must submit evidence satisfying **at least two (2)** of the following six categories:

### B1 — Major Prizes or Awards for Outstanding Achievement
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(A)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B1-P1`: Candidate received the prize or award.
  - `B1-P2`: The award is for outstanding achievement in the academic field.
  - `B1-P3`: The award is major (evaluated by criteria for receipt, national/international scope, significance of issuing body, number of recipients).

### B2 — Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievements
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(B)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B2-P1`: Candidate holds or held membership in the association in the academic field.
  - `B2-P2`: The specific membership grade requires outstanding achievements of its members.
  - `B2-P3`: Membership selection is judged by recognized national or international experts.

### B3 — Published Material Written by Others About Beneficiary's Work
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(C)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B3-P1`: Published material exists in professional publications.
  - `B3-P2`: The material was written by others (independent of the beneficiary).
  - `B3-P3`: The material is about the beneficiary's work in the academic field (not mere citation list or passing mention).
  - `B3-P4`: Required publication metadata (title, date, author, publication name, translation if applicable) is established.

### B4 — Judging the Work of Others
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(D)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B4-P1`: Candidate participated as a judge (individually or on a panel).
  - `B4-P2`: The activity involved judging the work of others.
  - `B4-P3`: The judging was in the same or an allied academic field.
  - `B4-P4`: The judging activity was actually completed (peer review of journal submissions, editorial board duties, dissertation committee, conference review).

### B5 — Original Scientific or Scholarly Research Contributions
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(E)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B5-P1`: Specific scientific or scholarly research contributions are identified.
  - `B5-P2`: The contributions are attributable to the candidate.
  - `B5-P3`: The contributions are original and in the academic field.
  - `B5-P4`: Evidence establishes that the contributions are of major significance in the academic field (widespread citation, replication, commercial or practical adoption, expert testimony).

### B6 — Authorship of Scholarly Books or Articles
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(i)(3)(i)(F)
- **Propositions:**
  - `B6-P1`: Candidate authored or co-authored scholarly books or articles.
  - `B6-P2`: The works are in the academic field.
  - `B6-P3`: The articles are published in scholarly journals with international circulation.

## 5. Final Merits Determination (B10)

- **Statutory Basis:** INA §203(b)(1)(B)(i)
- **Policy Basis:** USCIS Policy Manual Vol. 6, Part F, Ch. 3.B (*Two-Step Adjudication / Final Merits*)
- **Rule:** Meeting 2 of 6 criteria does not guarantee approval. The officer evaluates the totality of evidence to determine whether the beneficiary is **internationally recognized as outstanding in the academic field**.
- **Propositions:**
  - `B10-P1`: Totality of evidence establishes international standing and recognition above peers.
  - `B10-P2`: Sustained record of scholastic achievement and reputation in the academic discipline.
  - `B10-P3`: Evidence demonstrates that the candidate is regarded by the broader academic community as outstanding.
