# VisaPilot — EB-1C Evaluation Model

**Status:** Operational Evaluation Specification — Phase 1  
**Pathway:** EB-1C / E13 — Multinational Executives and Managers  
**Rule Version:** `EB1C-2026-09`

## 1. Gate-by-Gate Evaluation Architecture

```text
Step 1: Extract Corporate & Employment Facts
Step 2: Gate M1 — Foreign Employment (>= 12 continuous months in 3-yr window)
Step 3: Gate M2 — Corporate Qualifying Relationship (Parent/Sub/Affiliate)
Step 4: Gate M3 — U.S. Doing Business Requirement (>= 12 months operating)
Step 5: Gate M4 — Multinational Enterprise Active Operations
Step 6: Gate M5 — Foreign Capacity Evaluation (Executive / Personnel / Functional Manager)
Step 7: Gate M6 — U.S. Capacity & Job Offer Evaluation
Step 8: Blocker Identification & Gap Action Plan
```

## 2. Evaluation Logic for Managerial / Executive Capacity

### Personnel Manager Evaluation (INA §101(a)(44)(A))
1. Check organizational chart for subordinate employees.
2. Verify whether subordinate employees are **supervisors, managers, or professionals** (requiring at least a bachelor's degree).
3. First-line supervisors of non-professional workers are **excluded** under INA §101(a)(44)(C).
4. Verify authority over personnel actions (hire, fire, promote, grant leave).

### Functional Manager Evaluation (Matter of Z-A-, Inc.)
1. Verify candidate manages an **essential function** at a senior level.
2. Verify function is critical to the organization or a major division.
3. Verify candidate operates with high discretionary authority.
4. Verify non-managerial tasks are performed by other employees or contractors.

### Executive Capacity Evaluation (INA §101(a)(44)(B))
1. Verify candidate directs the management of the organization or major component.
2. Verify establishment of organizational goals and policies.
3. Verify wide latitude in discretionary decision-making.
4. Verify candidate receives only general supervision from higher executive tiers or board of directors.

## 3. Evaluation Rules for Corporate Structure

### Qualifying Relationship (M2)
- Must verify chain of ownership to the ultimate beneficial owners.
- Joint ventures require at least 50% ownership with negative control/veto rights.
- Contractual agreements, franchises, or licensing without common ownership are **non-qualifying**.

### Doing Business Requirement (M3)
- Must demonstrate active commercial operations in the U.S. for at least 12 months.
- A "shelf" company or holding company with no active business activity fails Gate M3.
