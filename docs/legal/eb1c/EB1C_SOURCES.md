## 1. Statutory Provisions

### INA §203(b)(1)(C), 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(C)
- **Authority:** Primary Statute
- **Title:** Certain Multinational Executives and Managers
- **Citation:** 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(C)
- **Key Terms:**
  - In the 3 years preceding application, employed for at least 1 year by a firm or corporation or other legal entity or an affiliate or subsidiary thereof;
  - Seeks to enter the U.S. to continue to render services to the same employer, or to a subsidiary or affiliate thereof, in a capacity that is managerial or executive.

### INA §101(a)(44), 8 U.S.C. §1101(a)(44)
- **Authority:** Primary Statute (Definitions)
- **Subsections:**
  - `INA §101(a)(44)(A)`: Definition of **Managerial Capacity** (Personnel manager & Functional manager).
  - `INA §101(a)(44)(B)`: Definition of **Executive Capacity**.
  - `INA §101(a)(44)(C)`: Staffing considerations (first-line supervisor rule).

## 2. Federal Regulations

### 8 CFR §204.5(j) — Certain Multinational Executives and Managers
- **Authority:** Binding Federal Regulation
- **Citation:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)
- **Key Subsections:**
  - `8 CFR §204.5(j)(1)`: Eligibility and petition scope.
  - `8 CFR §204.5(j)(2)`: Definitions:
    - *Affiliate*
    - *Doing business* (regular, systematic, and continuous provision of goods and/or services for at least 1 year)
    - *Executive capacity*
    - *Managerial capacity*
    - *Multinational*
    - *Subsidiary*
  - `8 CFR §204.5(j)(3)(i)`: Initial evidentiary requirements:
    - (A) Qualifying foreign employment for 1 year in the past 3 years outside the U.S.
    - (B) U.S. nonimmigrant exception (1 year within the 3 years preceding U.S. admission).
    - (C) Qualifying corporate relationship between U.S. and foreign entities.
    - (D) Prospective U.S. employer has been doing business for at least 1 year.
  - `8 CFR §204.5(j)(5)`: Job offer requirement (statement from authorized official describing managerial or executive duties).

## 3. USCIS Policy Manual

### USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 6, Part F, Chapter 4
- **Authority:** USCIS Administrative Policy
- **Title:** Multinational Executives and Managers (EB-1C)
- **Key Principles:**
  - Mandatory qualification across all statutory prongs;
  - Detailed examination of job duties vs high-level generic descriptions;
  - Scrutiny of organizational charts, staffing tiers, and subordinate professional personnel.
