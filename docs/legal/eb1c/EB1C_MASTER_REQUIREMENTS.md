## 1. Regulatory Foundation

EB-1C is **fundamentally different** from EB-1A and EB-1B.
- It is **not** an evidentiary points system (e.g. "3 of 10" or "2 of 6").
- It consists of **six mandatory statutory gates (M1–M6)**. Every single gate is mandatory. Failure of any gate creates an unresolved blocker.
- The petition requires a qualifying U.S. employer petitioner with a qualifying corporate relationship to the foreign employer.

Grounded in:
- **INA §203(b)(1)(C)**, 8 U.S.C. §1153(b)(1)(C)
- **INA §101(a)(44)** (Definitions of Managerial and Executive Capacity)
- **8 CFR §204.5(j)**
- **USCIS Policy Manual**, Volume 6, Part F, Chapter 4

## 2. The Six Mandatory Pathway Gates (M1–M6)

### M1: Qualifying Foreign Employment Duration & Timing
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)(3)(i)(A)–(B)
- **Rule:** The beneficiary must have been employed outside the United States for at least **1 continuous year within the 3 years** preceding the petition (or within the 3 years preceding entry if currently in the U.S. working for the qualifying entity).
- **Propositions:**
  - `M1-P1`: Beneficiary was employed outside the U.S. by the foreign entity for at least 1 continuous year (12 full months).
  - `M1-P2`: The foreign employment occurred within the relevant 3-year lookback window.
  - `M1-P3`: If beneficiary is in the U.S. under nonimmigrant status (e.g., L-1, H-1B), the 1-in-3 year qualifying employment preceded initial U.S. admission.

### M2: Qualifying Corporate Entity Relationship
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)(2), 8 CFR §204.5(j)(3)(i)(C)
- **Rule:** The prospective U.S. employer and the foreign employer must have a qualifying corporate relationship: **Same Employer, Parent/Subsidiary, or Affiliate**.
- **Definitions:**
  - *Same Employer:* Exact same legal entity operating branches.
  - *Parent / Subsidiary:* One entity owns directly or indirectly more than half of the other, or 50% of a 50/50 joint venture with equal control/veto, or owns directly/indirectly less than half but controls the entity.
  - *Affiliate:* Both entities owned and controlled by the same parent/individual or same group of individuals in approximately the same proportions.
- **Propositions:**
  - `M2-P1`: Common ownership and control exist between the U.S. petitioner and foreign entity.
  - `M2-P2`: Corporate documentation (articles of incorporation, stock certificates, capitalization tables, tax returns) substantiates the qualifying relationship.

### M3: U.S. Employer "Doing Business" for At Least 1 Year
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)(2), 8 CFR §204.5(j)(3)(i)(D)
- **Rule:** The petitioning U.S. employer must have been **doing business for at least 1 year** prior to filing.
- **Definition:** "Doing business" means the regular, systematic, and continuous provision of goods and/or services. Merely having an agent or office without active business operations does not qualify.
- **Propositions:**
  - `M3-P1`: U.S. petitioner has been actively engaged in regular, systematic, and continuous business for ≥ 12 months.
  - `M3-P2`: Substantiated by business licenses, payroll records, bank statements, commercial contracts, and tax filings.

### M4: Multinational Structure & Active Operations Abroad
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)(2)
- **Rule:** The petitioning enterprise must be multinational, doing business in the U.S. and at least one foreign country directly or through a qualifying relationship.
- **Propositions:**
  - `M4-P1`: The corporate enterprise actively conducts commercial operations in the U.S. and at least one other country.
  - `M4-P2`: The foreign entity remains an active, operating commercial business during the petition adjudication.

### M5: Qualifying Foreign Managerial or Executive Capacity
- **Regulatory Basis:** INA §101(a)(44), 8 CFR §204.5(j)(2)
- **Rule:** The beneficiary's 1-year qualifying employment abroad must have been primarily in a **Managerial Capacity** or **Executive Capacity**.
- **Two Distinct Evaluation Branches:**
  - **Branch A: Personnel Manager** — Manages the organization, department, subdivision, or component; supervises and controls the work of other supervisory, professional, or managerial employees; possesses authority to hire/fire or recommend personnel actions; exercises discretion over day-to-day operations.
  - **Branch B: Functional Manager** — Manages an essential function at a high level within the organizational hierarchy; does not necessarily supervise staff, but function is critical; operates under general supervision of executive/board; exercises discretion.
  - **Branch C: Executive Capacity** — Directs the management of the organization or a major component; establishes goals and policies; exercises wide latitude in discretionary decision-making; receives only general supervision from higher-level executives, board of directors, or stockholders.
- **Propositions:**
  - `M5-P1`: Foreign job duties are described in detail with percentage time breakdowns.
  - `M5-P2`: Duties were primarily managerial or executive, rather than performing day-to-day operational or non-qualifying tasks.
  - `M5-P3`: Organizational chart substantiates subordinate reporting tiers (professionals, managers, support staff).

### M6: Qualifying U.S. Position & Permanent Job Offer
- **Regulatory Basis:** 8 CFR §204.5(j)(5)
- **Rule:** The prospective U.S. position must be full-time, permanent, and primarily in a **Managerial or Executive Capacity**.
- **Propositions:**
  - `M6-P1`: Full-time, permanent job offer from the petitioning U.S. employer.
  - `M6-P2`: Detailed description of prospective U.S. duties demonstrating primarily managerial or executive responsibilities.
  - `M6-P3`: U.S. staffing structure (organizational chart, subordinate headcount, wages) demonstrates that non-managerial operational duties are relieved by subordinate staff.
